# Data release policy for STEP F

Release scope is **software + project-owned configuration/QA/reproduction artifacts**, not republication of third-party historical benchmark datasets.

The Copenhagen and Hanford raw benchmark files are not redistributed and remain excluded because explicit redistribution permission has not been established in the frozen project record. Exclusion is a rights/provenance control, not a claim that the benchmark sources are secret or inaccessible. Provenance, authoritative acquisition routes, checksums, and project-owned derived audit artifacts may be released where those artifacts do not reproduce protected raw data.

A later rights-clearance decision may create a separate canonical-data package. That decision is not required to falsify or weaken the present code release; it must simply be described accurately in the manuscript and repository.
